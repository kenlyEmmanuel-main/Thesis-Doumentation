{\fontsize{14pt}{17pt}\selectfont\textbf{Abstract}}
\vspace{1\baselineskip}
\end{center}

\begin{singlespace}
\noindent
Public bidding in the Philippines requires prospective contractors to prepare and submit extensive, multi-hundred-page compliance dossiers under rigid statutory timelines and a non-discretionary pass/fail standard.
Clerical omissions, template drift, and accidental visual occlusion of signatures or notary seals frequently lead to immediate disqualification.
This study presents the architectural design and empirical evaluation framework of an offline, edge-deployable document intelligence system tailored to Philippine Bidding Documents (PBDs) under Republic Act No. 9184 and Republic Act No. 12009.
The proposed system couples an ONNX-optimized YOLOv8s single-stage object detector for semantic document layout segmentation with an in-memory RapidOCR (PP-OCRv3) engine and a deterministic statutory rule evaluator.
To prevent document mutilation, an active dodging engine evaluates two-dimensional margin clearance to dynamically affix pagination, endorsements, and institutional stamps without occluding pre-existing text or legal marks.
Operating strictly within an air-gapped local environment, the system safeguards proprietary trade secrets and confidential financial data in compliance with the Data Privacy Act of 2012.

\vspace{2\baselineskip}

\noindent\textbf{Keywords (maximum of five):} document intelligence, layout segmentation, YOLOv8, public procurement, obstacle avoidance
\end{singlespace}
